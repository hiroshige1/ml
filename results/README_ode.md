# Population-ODE numbers: adaptive Euler (1% step) vs LSODA

Generated by `scripts/ode_lsoda.py` (do not edit by hand). LSODA: `scipy.integrate.solve_ivp(method="LSODA", rtol=1e-10, atol=1e-14)`, terminal event at m = 0.5 (root-located), trap = initial drift <= 0 or drift sign change before m = 0.5 (the Euler scripts' rule). LSODA agrees with DOP853 and Radau (rtol 1e-12) to ~1e-8 relative on six spot-checked cells. Euler values are obtained by calling the existing functions (`T05` of `ode_kappa_exp1b.py`; `time_to` of `ode_two_timescale.py`; `run` of `ode_tied.py`). rel_diff = (LSODA - Euler)/Euler. Files: `results/ode_lsoda.csv` (all 300+ computed quantities), `results/ode_lsoda_quoted.csv` (each quoted number).

## Summary

- Largest relative difference over all 301 finite quantities: **3.55%** (T05_steps_d64, d=64;pinned0.1;N=16;steps=flow*4096). Over the quoted numbers only: 3.55% (pinned 0.1 ODE steps, N=16).
- Euler **overestimates** every escape time (all 177 finite-time comparisons have LSODA < Euler), confirming the reviewer: free-readout flows by 0.6-1.8%, tied by 1.5-2.3%, pinned by 1.5-3.6%; the largest shifts are at the trap boundary (pinned 0.1, d=64, N=16: 3.55%; d=512, N=128: 3.35%).
- Dimensionless quantities are essentially unchanged because the bias is nearly a constant factor: every kappa_eff moves by <= 0.004 (<0.07%), every d-slope by <= 0.004, T(16)/T(256) ratios by <= 2.0% (pinned 0.1) and <= 0.9% otherwise. Trap/escape verdicts (inf entries) are identical in all cells.
- Net: absolute escape times / step counts shift by 0.6-3.6%; no exponent, ratio, trap verdict or qualitative conclusion changes.
